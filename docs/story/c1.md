# C1: *Her own studio*

Último nivel de la historia (ver [bible.md](bible.md)). Va de julio del tercer año a octubre del cuarto. Lucía y Daniel se van a vivir juntos, Helen se despide, nace Green Door Design, llegan la primera clienta (Grace Walker) y la presentación ante Mark Reed en Nueva York, Priya se compromete y Lucía, al recoger un premio, mira atrás a su primer día en Bristol.

**Formato**: el mismo que en [a1.md](a1.md).

---

## 1. What keeps me awake
`unit: the_offer`

> July, year three. Lucía and Daniel have just moved into a flat near the harbour. There are boxes everywhere, and Lucía hasn't slept properly since Helen's offer.

**Daniel:** You've been staring at that ceiling for an hour. What's on your mind?
**Lucía:** What I keep asking myself is whether I'm ready.
**Daniel:** Ready for what, exactly? Running a studio, or saying no to Mark?
**Lucía:** It's not Mark I'm worried about. It's the clients. What if they follow Helen's name, not mine?
**Daniel:** The thing is, they already ask for you. It was you who saved the museum project, not Helen.
**Lucía:** It was both of us. What Helen gave me was the chance.
**Daniel:** And what you did with it was impressive. All I'm saying is, don't let fear decide for you.
**Lucía:** What scares me most is the money. I'd be risking everything we've saved.
**Daniel:** The reason I'm not worried is that you never do anything without a plan.
**Lucía:** That's true. Where I'd really need help is the technical side.
**Daniel:** Then it's me you need. I'm not going anywhere.
**Lucía:** Are you offering to leave Harbour Studio?
**Daniel:** What I'm offering is a partner. If you'll have me.

## 2. Helen's farewell
`unit: inversion_emphasis`

> December. Harbour Studio's farewell party for Helen. The office is full of former clients, colleagues and friends. Helen taps her glass, and the room goes quiet.

**Helen:** Twenty years ago, I started this studio at my kitchen table. Little did I know how far it would go.
**Helen:** Never have I worked with a team as talented, or as stubborn, as this one.
**Helen:** Not only did you make our clients happy, but you also made me laugh every single day.
**Helen:** Rarely does a boss get to say that, so I'll say it twice. Thank you.
**Helen:** Now, let's address the elephant in the room. Yes, Mark Reed offered to buy us. And no, I didn't accept.
**Mark:** And I'm still disappointed, Helen!
**Helen:** Only when I saw what Lucía could do did I understand what the future of this studio should look like.
**Helen:** Losing a few clients to her new studio will be a blessing in disguise. They'll be in excellent hands.
**Lucía:** Helen, you're going to make me cry.
**Helen:** Under no circumstances are you to cry, Lucía. Not tonight.
**Helen:** And if anyone tells you I'm retiring because I'm tired, take it with a pinch of salt. I'm retiring because I want to sail around Greece.
**Helen:** No sooner had I booked the boat than my husband bought a captain's hat. Please, raise your glasses. To Harbour Studio!

## 3. Above the bookshop
`unit: starting_up`

> January, year four. Lucía and Daniel are standing in an empty office above a bookshop on Park Street, with an estate agent.

**Agent:** Built in 1890, the building has been completely renovated. Having been empty for six months, the office is available immediately.
**Lucía:** It's lovely. Looking out of this window, I can see the street where I got lost on my first Saturday in Bristol.
**Daniel:** Next to the café, opposite the cinema. I gave you directions, remember?
**Lucía:** How could I forget?
**Agent:** If signed today, the contract would allow you to move in on Monday.
**Daniel:** Having seen six other offices, I'd say this is the best by far.
**Lucía:** Agreed. Not being an expert in property, though, I'd like to read the contract carefully first.
**Agent:** Of course. Taken as a whole, the terms are very reasonable.
**Lucía:** I'll call you this afternoon, having read every word.
**Daniel:** So, what are we calling the company?
**Lucía:** Inspired by the first person who welcomed me to Bristol… Green Door Design.
**Daniel:** After Mrs Green's front door? She'll be thrilled.
**Agent:** I'll leave you two to talk. Congratulations, by the way.

## 4. The first client
`unit: first_client`

> February. Green Door Design has its first meeting with a potential client: Grace Walker, an Australian businesswoman who runs a chain of eco-friendly hostels.

**Grace:** I'll be honest with you. It would appear that your prices are rather higher than your competitors'.
**Lucía:** That may well be the case. However, our designs tend to bring in considerably more bookings.
**Grace:** Arguably, that depends on the market. Our guests are mostly students.
**Daniel:** To some extent, yes. But there seems to be a clear link between good design and trust, even among younger travellers.
**Grace:** I'm not entirely convinced. Do you have any evidence?
**Lucía:** We'd suggest a small pilot. It might be worth testing one hostel first.
**Grace:** That sounds reasonable, I suppose. What would that cost?
**Lucía:** Roughly a quarter of the full project. If the results are good, we'd continue.
**Grace:** It seems to me that you're quite confident.
**Daniel:** Cautiously confident, let's say.
**Grace:** I like that. Let's give it a go.

## 5. The first hire
`unit: first_hire`

> March. The pilot was a success, and Green Door Design needs its first employee. Lucía and Daniel are reading applications in the office.

**Daniel:** We needn't have advertised, you know. Helen sent us three excellent CVs yesterday.
**Lucía:** I know. And I didn't need to pay for that expensive job advert either. Lesson learned.
**Daniel:** Who would you rather hire, the illustrator or the junior developer?
**Lucía:** I'd rather we hired a designer first. We've got more design work than code.
**Daniel:** Fair enough. But we'd better decide soon. Grace wants the full project by May.
**Lucía:** Then we'd better not waste any time. Let's interview both tomorrow.
**Daniel:** You needn't come to the interviews if you're busy. I can do them.
**Lucía:** I'd rather be there. It's our first hire; it has to feel right.
**Daniel:** Should we have asked Priya? She knows half of Bristol.
**Lucía:** She'd have told us to hire someone who cooks, like Ollie.
**Daniel:** We'd better not tell her we've got a coffee machine, or she'll apply herself.

## 6. Terms and conditions
`unit: money_talk`

> April. Grace is so happy with the results that she wants to invest in Green Door Design. Lucía and Daniel meet her to discuss the terms.

**Grace:** I'm prepared to invest, provided that I get a seat on the board.
**Lucía:** As long as we keep creative control, that seems fair.
**Grace:** You'll keep it, on condition that the studio grows by thirty percent next year.
**Daniel:** Supposing we don't reach thirty percent, what happens then?
**Grace:** Then we'd sit down and talk. Unless something drastic happened, I wouldn't pull out.
**Lucía:** But for your pilot project, we'd never have got this far.
**Grace:** And but for your designs, my bookings wouldn't have doubled.
**Daniel:** There's one more thing. Mark Reed has invited us to pitch in New York in May.
**Grace:** Go. Otherwise, you'll always wonder. Should he make an offer, though, tell me first.
**Lucía:** Of course. Assuming everything goes well, we'll be back with good news.
**Grace:** In that case, I'll draw up the contract.

## 7. The night before
`unit: pitch_prep`

> May. New York, the night before the pitch. Lucía and Daniel are in their hotel room, rewriting the presentation for Mark Reed's board.

**Daniel:** Slide four says: "We grew really fast last year." Too casual?
**Lucía:** Much too casual. Let's say: "The studio experienced rapid growth last year."
**Daniel:** Fine. Slide five: "Clients booked more rooms because the sites were easier to use."
**Lucía:** "Improved usability led to a significant increase in bookings."
**Daniel:** You sound like Helen. Slide six: "We want to open an office in Madrid."
**Lucía:** "Our intention is to expand into the Spanish market." Mark loves the word "expand".
**Daniel:** And this one: "We decided to work with Grace because she understood us."
**Lucía:** "Our decision to partner with Grace was based on a shared vision."
**Daniel:** Do people really talk like this?
**Lucía:** In boardrooms they do. Tomorrow we speak their language. Tonight, we speak ours.
**Daniel:** In that case, my proposal is the immediate acquisition of a pizza.
**Lucía:** Proposal accepted.

## 8. The pitch
`unit: the_pitch`

> The next morning, in Reed Hotels' boardroom, forty floors above Manhattan. Lucía has just finished presenting. Mark leans back in his chair.

**Mark:** I do have to say, that was impressive.
**Lucía:** Thank you. We did prepare quite carefully.
**Mark:** Such a small studio, and yet such ambitious plans. What surprises me is the Madrid office.
**Lucía:** Madrid is where I started my career. I do know that market very well.
**Mark:** So convincing were your numbers that my finance director stopped taking notes.
**Daniel:** The numbers are real, Mark. Every single one.
**Mark:** Oh, I don't doubt it. What I'm proposing is simple: we invest, and you design all our European hotels.
**Lucía:** That's a very generous offer. It does come with conditions, I assume?
**Mark:** Just one. Grace Walker stays on board. I've heard such good things about her.
**Lucía:** That we can promise. Grace is the reason we're here.
**Mark:** Then it's a deal. And Lucía? Helen was right about you.
**Lucía:** Helen usually is.

## 9. Jet lag
`unit: jet_lag`

> Back in Bristol, still jet-lagged, Lucía calls Javi in Dublin to tell him everything.

**Javi:** So? You were going to call me on Tuesday!
**Lucía:** I know, sorry. I was about to call you when Mark's assistant rang about the contract.
**Javi:** And? Was it good news?
**Lucía:** It was. We were supposed to have a short meeting, but it lasted four hours.
**Javi:** Four hours! Did you know it would go so well?
**Lucía:** Not at all. I was sure Mark would say no.
**Javi:** And now? What happens next?
**Lucía:** The Madrid office was going to open next year, but now it'll open in October.
**Javi:** Madrid! So you'll be living in Spain again?
**Lucía:** Half the time. When I left Valencia, I had no idea I was to come back as a business owner.
**Javi:** Mum is going to cry. You were only going to stay in England for one year, remember?
**Lucía:** I remember. Three years later, here I am.

## 10. The engagement party
`unit: the_engagement`

> June. Priya has just got engaged to Arjun, a doctor from her hospital, and there's a party at Clifton Road. Sam is back from his European tour.

**Sam:** Priya! Congratulations! Did you know he was going to propose?
**Priya:** I hoped so, but I didn't expect it to happen in the middle of the hospital.
**Lucía:** In the hospital? I'd have loved to see that.
**Priya:** So would half the nurses. They all came out to watch.
**Sam:** Are you having a big wedding?
**Priya:** I think so. My mum wants five hundred guests. I'd rather not.
**Ollie:** Who's cooking? Me, I hope.
**Priya:** If you'd like to.
**Ollie:** I would. Indian and Spanish fusion, like this house.
**Sam:** And the band could play, if you want us to.
**Priya:** Of course I want you to! Lucía, you'll be my bridesmaid, won't you?
**Lucía:** Of course I will. Have you chosen a date?
**Priya:** Not yet. Next summer, probably. Have you and Daniel?
**Lucía:** Have we what?
**Priya:** Chosen a date!
**Lucía:** We're not engaged, Priya! Are we, Daniel?
**Daniel:** Not yet.

## 11. On the radio
`unit: radio_interview`

> August. Lucía is being interviewed on a Bristol radio programme about small businesses.

**Interviewer:** Green Door Design is barely a year old, and yet you're about to open an office in Madrid. How did that happen?
**Lucía:** Partly luck, partly hard work. We were lucky to find the right partners early on.
**Interviewer:** The Madrid office is due to open in October. Is it on schedule?
**Lucía:** It is. We're set to hire four designers there by the end of the year.
**Interviewer:** Are you likely to move to Madrid yourself?
**Lucía:** I'm unlikely to move permanently. Bristol is home now. But I'm bound to spend a lot of time on planes.
**Interviewer:** Some say small studios are on the verge of disappearing because of AI tools. Do you agree?
**Lucía:** I don't think so. Good design is bound to need people who listen. Tools can help, but they don't understand a client's fears.
**Interviewer:** What are your chances of winning the Bristol Business Award this year?
**Lucía:** We stand a fairly good chance, I'm told. But honestly, I'd be just as happy without it.
**Interviewer:** Finally, any advice for someone arriving in a new city, as you did three years ago?
**Lucía:** Say yes to everything, carry an umbrella, and never keep your keys in the fridge.

## 12. Home
`unit: looking_back`

> October. Green Door Design has just won the Bristol Business Award. Everyone is there: Daniel, Priya, Sam, Ollie, Mrs Green, Helen, and Javi, who has flown in from Dublin. Lucía walks up to the stage.

**Lucía:** Three years ago, I arrived on Clifton Road with a suitcase, a Spanish fan and very little English.
**Lucía:** I had been studying English for months, but I couldn't understand a word the bus driver said.
**Lucía:** Mrs Green, who's here tonight, gave me a key and told me I'd be fine. She was right.
**Lucía:** In those first weeks, I would get lost every Saturday, and Daniel would give me directions. I used to think "craic" was a kind of bread.
**Lucía:** Sam lent me his grandmother's lamp and an umbrella, which the wind destroyed a month later.
**Lucía:** Priya taught me that tea solves almost everything, and Ollie proved that good soup solves the rest.
**Lucía:** And Helen, who had been running Harbour Studio for twenty years, gave a nervous Spanish designer her first real chance.
**Lucía:** Little did I know that this city would become my home. Thank you, Bristol.
**Daniel:** That was perfect. Come outside for a minute. I've been waiting all night to ask you something.
**Lucía:** Daniel… what's in your hand?
**Daniel:** Lucía Martín, will you marry me?
**Lucía:** Yes! Of course I will!
**Javi:** I knew it! I told Mum two years ago!
