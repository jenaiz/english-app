# B1: *The Irish trip*

Tercer nivel de la historia (ver [bible.md](bible.md)). Va de enero a agosto del segundo año. Lucía y Daniel empiezan a salir, ella consigue el puesto de diseñadora sénior, viajan a Dublín y Galway, Sam se va a Londres a grabar un disco y llega un compañero de piso nuevo, Ollie.

**Formato**: el mismo que en [a1.md](a1.md). Si una unidad tiene más de una lección, el capítulo indica la lección: `` `unit: <unit_id>/<lesson_id>` ``.

---

## 1. A first date
`unit: first_date`

> January, year two. Lucía is back from Valencia with a suntan and a suitcase full of turrón. On Friday night, she meets Daniel at a small Irish restaurant near the harbour.

**Daniel:** You look great. How was Valencia?
**Lucía:** Wonderful. I ate too much and slept a lot. My mum still thinks I'm twelve!
**Daniel:** Ha! Mine too. When I go home, she makes me eat three dinners.
**Lucía:** So tell me, what were you like as a child?
**Daniel:** Oh, I used to be very shy. I didn't use to talk to anyone at school.
**Lucía:** You? Shy? I don't believe it!
**Daniel:** It's true. I used to hide in my room and read books about computers. What about you?
**Lucía:** I used to dance flamenco… badly! And I used to want to be a pilot.
**Daniel:** A pilot? Why did you change your mind?
**Lucía:** I used to get sick on planes. I still do, actually!
**Daniel:** Well, I'm glad you flew to Bristol anyway.
**Lucía:** Me too. This is the best restaurant I've been to here.
**Daniel:** Then we'll have to come again.

## 2. Since January
`unit: since_january`

> February. Everyone at Clifton Road seems tired. Lucía comes home from work and finds Priya asleep on her books and Sam playing the guitar in the kitchen.

**Lucía:** Priya, wake up! How long have you been studying?
**Priya:** Since six o'clock this morning. I've been preparing for my exam for weeks.
**Lucía:** What exam is it?
**Priya:** It's to become a senior nurse. I've wanted this job for years.
**Sam:** And I've been practising this song since lunchtime. Listen!
**Lucía:** It sounds great, but you've been playing it all week!
**Sam:** That's because we've got a big gig on Saturday. A music producer is coming.
**Lucía:** A producer? That's amazing!
**Priya:** And you, Lucía? You look happy. Have you been seeing a certain Irishman?
**Lucía:** We've been out three times since January.
**Sam:** Three times! Is it serious?
**Lucía:** Maybe. He's been teaching me Irish words. "Grand", "craic", "eejit"…
**Priya:** "Eejit"? That's what we call Sam!

## 3. The interview
`unit: job_interview`

> March. Harbour Studio is growing, and Helen needs a senior designer. Lucía has applied for the job, and today is her interview.

**Helen:** So, Lucía, you've worked here for six months now. What have you learned?
**Lucía:** A lot. When I arrived, I didn't know anyone, and it took me a few weeks to learn the ropes. Now I feel at home.
**Helen:** Have you ever managed a team?
**Lucía:** Yes, I have. In Madrid I managed three junior designers for a year.
**Helen:** When did you finish that job?
**Lucía:** I left in August, just before I moved to Bristol.
**Helen:** And what's the most difficult project you've worked on here?
**Lucía:** The museum website. We had a very short deadline, so I worked late every night that week.
**Helen:** Yes, I remember. You really went the extra mile.
**Lucía:** Thank you. I've always enjoyed a challenge.
**Helen:** The senior designer will have to take on two new clients. Are you ready for that?
**Lucía:** Absolutely. I've been waiting for an opportunity like this.
**Helen:** Good. I'll let you know next week.

## 4. Flight to Dublin
`unit: airport_travel`

> April, Easter holidays. Lucía got the senior job, and she and Daniel are flying to Dublin to celebrate. But they set off late, and the traffic to the airport is terrible.

**Daniel:** We've made it! Just in the nick of time.
**Agent:** Good morning. Passports, please. Are you checking in any bags?
**Lucía:** Yes, one suitcase.
**Agent:** Your bag will be sent directly to Dublin. Did you pack it yourself?
**Lucía:** Yes, I did.
**Agent:** I'm afraid it's too heavy. Bags over twenty-three kilos are charged an extra fee.
**Daniel:** How much is it?
**Agent:** Thirty pounds. Your boarding passes have been sent to your phones. Boarding will begin at gate seven at ten forty.
**Agent:** Attention, please. Flight EI six five three to Dublin has been delayed by thirty minutes. Passengers are asked to wait in the departure lounge.
**Lucía:** Delayed? After all that running!
**Daniel:** Never mind. I'm really looking forward to showing you Dublin.
**Lucía:** And I'm looking forward to meeting your mam. I'm a bit nervous, too!
**Daniel:** Don't be. She's been asking about you since Christmas.

## 5. The Liffey House Hotel
`unit: hotel_checkin/phrasal_verbs_hotel`

> That evening they arrive at a small hotel in the centre of Dublin, near the River Liffey.

**Receptionist:** Good evening, welcome to the Liffey House Hotel. Are you checking in?
**Daniel:** Yes, we have a reservation under O'Brien.
**Receptionist:** Lovely. Could you fill in this form, please? And I'll need to see your passports.
**Lucía:** Of course. Here you are.
**Receptionist:** Thank you. Breakfast is served from seven till ten, and check-out is at eleven.
**Daniel:** Can we drop off our bags here tomorrow after we check out? Our train to Galway is at three.
**Receptionist:** No problem, we'll look after them. Just pick them up before you leave.
**Lucía:** Is the room quiet? I'm a light sleeper.
**Receptionist:** Well, it faces the street, so it can be a bit noisy at weekends.
**Daniel:** Don't worry, we can put up with a bit of noise.
**Receptionist:** Room two one four, second floor. The lift is on your left. Enjoy your stay!

## 6. A polite complaint
`unit: hotel_checkin/hotel_complaints`

> The next morning, Lucía goes down to reception. She hasn't slept much.

**Lucía:** Good morning. I'm sorry to bother you, but I'm afraid there's a problem with our room.
**Receptionist:** Oh dear. What seems to be the problem?
**Lucía:** Well, the street was very noisy until three. Then this morning the shower didn't work.
**Receptionist:** I'm so sorry about that.
**Lucía:** There was no hot water at all. Honestly, that was the last straw.
**Receptionist:** I completely understand. Would you like to move to a quieter room?
**Lucía:** We're leaving today, actually. But would you mind sending someone to look at the shower?
**Receptionist:** Not at all. And let me take ten percent off your bill.
**Lucía:** That's very kind of you. Thank you.
**Receptionist:** Could I offer you a free breakfast too?
**Lucía:** Oh, that would be lovely. Daniel will be very happy about that!

## 7. Dinner in Galway
`unit: galway_family`

> Two days later, in Galway. Daniel's mother, Maureen, has made a huge dinner. Outside, the rain is hitting the windows.

**Maureen:** Lucía, love, have some more potatoes. If you don't eat, I'll be offended!
**Lucía:** Thank you, Maureen. If I eat any more, I won't be able to move!
**Daniel:** Mam, leave her alone. So, what are we doing tomorrow?
**Maureen:** If the weather's good, you should take her to the Cliffs of Moher.
**Daniel:** And if it rains?
**Maureen:** If it rains, you'll get wet! This is Ireland, Daniel.
**Lucía:** I don't mind the rain. I've got my red umbrella.
**Maureen:** Good girl. And when you come back, I'll make my famous apple tart.
**Daniel:** As soon as we get back, I'll put the kettle on.
**Maureen:** Lucía, will you come again in the summer?
**Lucía:** I'd love to. If Daniel invites me!
**Daniel:** Unless she gets tired of me first.
**Maureen:** Sure, she won't get tired of you. You're a grand lad.

## 8. A big decision
`unit: big_decision`

> May. Sam comes home with big news. The music producer from February wants his band to record an album in London.

**Sam:** They want us in London for six months. Six months!
**Priya:** Sam, that's amazing! So why do you look so worried?
**Sam:** If I went, I'd have to leave the pub, and the house. And you two.
**Lucía:** If I were you, I'd go. It's your dream.
**Sam:** But what would I do if it didn't work?
**Priya:** If it didn't work, you'd come back. Mrs Green would give you your room again.
**Sam:** Would you miss me?
**Priya:** Miss you? If you left, the kitchen would be much tidier!
**Lucía:** We'd miss you a lot. Who would make chicken soup when I'm ill?
**Sam:** OK. If you promise to visit me, I'll go.
**Lucía:** Of course we'll visit!
**Priya:** But we'll need a new flatmate…

## 9. House rules
`unit: house_rules`

> June. Mrs Green comes to Clifton Road to talk about finding a new flatmate before Sam leaves.

**Mrs Green:** Now, the new flatmate must be clean and quiet. That's the first rule.
**Priya:** Of course. They'll have to share the kitchen with us.
**Mrs Green:** And they mustn't smoke in the house. Nobody smokes in my houses.
**Lucía:** Can they have a pet? I'd love a cat.
**Mrs Green:** No, I'm afraid you can't have pets. The neighbours are allergic.
**Sam:** Do they have to pay a deposit?
**Mrs Green:** Yes. Everyone has to pay one month's rent as a deposit. You did too, Sam!
**Sam:** And can they play music?
**Mrs Green:** Not after eleven o'clock. You know that better than anyone!
**Priya:** Do we need to put an advert online?
**Mrs Green:** You don't need to. My nephew Ollie is looking for a room. He's a chef.
**Lucía:** A chef? Then he must cook for us!
**Mrs Green:** Ha! Well, you can ask him.

## 10. The new flatmate
`unit: new_flatmate`

> A week later, Ollie comes to see the room. He's twenty-six, he's from Manchester, and he works in a restaurant which opened last year in Bristol.

**Ollie:** Hi! I'm Ollie, Mrs Green's nephew.
**Priya:** Welcome, Ollie. This is the kitchen, where we spend most of our time.
**Lucía:** And this is the garden that Priya looks after. Those are her famous tomatoes.
**Ollie:** Wow. And who's the guy who's singing upstairs?
**Priya:** That's Sam, the flatmate who's leaving. His room is the one you'd have.
**Ollie:** Is it the room which looks over the garden?
**Lucía:** Yes, that's it. It's the best room in the house.
**Ollie:** What's the area like? Is there a place where I can go running?
**Lucía:** Brandon Hill is five minutes away. It's the park where we play football.
**Ollie:** Perfect. By the way, I work at a restaurant that serves Spanish food.
**Lucía:** Spanish food? Then you're the flatmate we've been looking for!
**Priya:** When can you move in?
**Ollie:** At the beginning of August, when Sam leaves.

## 11. Good news
`unit: good_news`

> July. Lucía runs into Daniel's office after a meeting with Helen.

**Daniel:** You look excited. What did Helen want?
**Lucía:** She said that the museum loved our website!
**Daniel:** Brilliant! What else did she say?
**Lucía:** She told me that she wanted me to speak at a design conference in Edinburgh in October.
**Daniel:** Speak? In front of hundreds of people?
**Lucía:** I know! I told her I was nervous, but she said I would be great.
**Daniel:** She's right. You'll be grand.
**Lucía:** She also asked if you could come and help with the technical part.
**Daniel:** Me? In Edinburgh? Sounds good to me.
**Lucía:** And there's more news. Javi called this morning. He said he had passed his English exam!
**Daniel:** Fair play to him! What's he going to do now?
**Lucía:** He told me he had got a job at a hotel in Dublin. He starts in September.
**Daniel:** Dublin! My mam will want to feed him every Sunday.

## 12. The farewell gig
`unit: farewell_gig`

> Saturday night was Sam's farewell gig at The Old Duke. Priya missed it because she was working, so on Sunday morning Lucía tells her everything.

**Priya:** So, how was it? I'm so sorry I missed it.
**Lucía:** It was crazy! When we arrived, the band had already started.
**Priya:** Already? Why were you late?
**Lucía:** Daniel had left the tickets at home, so we had to go back.
**Priya:** Typical! And how was Sam?
**Lucía:** Nervous at first. He had never played in front of so many people.
**Priya:** Was the producer there?
**Lucía:** Yes, he had come from London to see them. And in the middle of the concert, the electricity went off!
**Priya:** No! What did they do?
**Lucía:** Luckily, Sam had brought his old guitar, so he played without electricity. Everyone was singing!
**Priya:** That's so Sam. Did he cry?
**Lucía:** A bit. He said it was the best night of his life.

## 13. Summer plans
`unit: summer_plans`

> August. It's Sam's last day at Clifton Road. There's a van outside, and everyone is helping him to carry boxes.

**Sam:** Right, that's the last box. The van's leaving at twelve.
**Priya:** What time are you meeting the band in London?
**Sam:** We're meeting at the studio at five. We're starting the recording on Monday.
**Lucía:** Are you going to visit us at Christmas?
**Sam:** Definitely. And I'll be back for Priya's birthday too, I promise.
**Daniel:** And what about you two? Any summer plans, Priya?
**Priya:** I'm working most of August. But I might go to Cornwall for a few days.
**Lucía:** And we're flying to Valencia on the fifteenth. Daniel is meeting my parents!
**Sam:** Ha! Is he nervous?
**Daniel:** Nervous? Me? Maybe a bit. I think they'll like me. I might cook them an Irish stew.
**Lucía:** In August? In Valencia? You might die of heat!
**Ollie:** Hi, everyone! I'm moving in a bit early. Is that OK?
**Sam:** Perfect timing. Look after my room, mate. And look after these two.
**Ollie:** I will. Good luck in London!
