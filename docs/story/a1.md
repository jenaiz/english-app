# A1: *Arriving in Bristol*

Primer nivel de la historia (ver [bible.md](bible.md)). Cada capítulo es el contexto (`audio_context`) de una unidad. La escena (`>`) la lee el Narrador y el diálogo lo dice cada personaje con su voz.

**Formato** (lo usa el generador de contenido, no hay que cambiarlo): `## N. Título`, una línea `` `unit: <unit_id>` ``, la escena con `>` y las líneas `**Speaker:** texto`.

---

## 1. Hello, Bristol
`unit: saying_hello`

> It's Saturday evening. This is Clifton Road, in Bristol. Lucía is at number fourteen with a big suitcase. She's from Spain and she's very tired.

**Mrs Green:** Hello! Are you Lucía?
**Lucía:** Yes, I am. Hello!
**Mrs Green:** I'm Mrs Green. Welcome to Bristol!
**Lucía:** Thank you. Nice to meet you.
**Mrs Green:** Nice to meet you too. Where are you from, Lucía?
**Lucía:** I'm from Spain. I'm from Valencia.
**Mrs Green:** Oh, lovely! Are you tired?
**Lucía:** Yes, I am. I'm very tired!
**Mrs Green:** Come in, come in. We're all friends here. Here's your key.
**Lucía:** Thank you, Mrs Green!

## 2. The flatmates
`unit: new_flatmates`

> Lucía is in the kitchen. A man and a woman are at the table. They're her new flatmates.

**Sam:** Hiya! You're Lucía, right? I'm Sam.
**Lucía:** Hi, Sam. Nice to meet you.
**Sam:** And this is Priya. She's a nurse.
**Priya:** Hello, Lucía. Welcome to the house!
**Lucía:** Thanks! Are you a nurse too, Sam?
**Sam:** No, I'm not. I'm a musician. And a waiter!
**Priya:** His band is really good. They're called The Clifton Cats.
**Lucía:** Cool! Is your family in Bristol, Priya?
**Priya:** No, they aren't. My parents are in Leicester. Is your family in Spain?
**Lucía:** Yes, they are. My parents are in Valencia, and my brother, Javi.
**Sam:** Is he a student?
**Lucía:** Yes, he is. He's twenty-five, and he's very funny.

## 3. Unpacking
`unit: numbers_colors`

> Lucía's room is small, but it's nice. Her suitcase is open on the bed. There's a knock at the door.

**Sam:** Knock, knock! Is this a good time?
**Lucía:** Yes, come in!
**Sam:** These are for you. Two towels and a lamp.
**Lucía:** Oh, thank you! Is that an old lamp?
**Sam:** Yes, it's my grandma's lamp. It's a bit ugly!
**Lucía:** No, it's a nice green lamp.
**Sam:** And what are those?
**Lucía:** These? They're photos of my family. And this is a fan, a Spanish fan for hot days.
**Sam:** Hot days? In Bristol? Ha! You need an umbrella, not a fan!
**Lucía:** Is that an umbrella?
**Sam:** Yes. It's a big black umbrella. And it's for you!

## 4. The first day
`unit: first_day`

> It's Monday morning. It's half past eight. This is Harbour Studio, an office near the harbour. Lucía is the new designer.

**Helen:** Good morning! You're Lucía Martín, right? I'm Helen Carter.
**Lucía:** Good morning, Helen. Nice to meet you.
**Helen:** And this is Daniel. He's a developer. He's from Ireland.
**Daniel:** Hi, Lucía! How are you?
**Lucía:** I'm fine, thanks. Where in Ireland are you from?
**Daniel:** Galway. It's a grand place on the west coast.
**Helen:** Have you got your laptop, Lucía?
**Lucía:** Yes, I have. What's my desk number?
**Daniel:** It's fourteen. It's next to my desk.
**Lucía:** Fourteen? My house number is fourteen too!
**Daniel:** Ha! That's lucky. What time is the meeting, Helen?
**Helen:** It's at half past nine. And lunch is at one o'clock.
**Daniel:** And Friday is pizza day!

## 5. Dinner at The Old Duke
`unit: at_the_pub`

> It's Friday evening. Lucía, Sam and Priya are at The Old Duke, a pub near the harbour. It's busy and there's music.

**Waiter:** Hi there! What can I get you?
**Priya:** Can I have a lemonade, please?
**Sam:** A cider for me, please.
**Lucía:** Is there any wine?
**Waiter:** Yes, there is. There's red wine and white wine.
**Lucía:** A glass of red wine, please.
**Sam:** Are there any vegetarian dishes? Priya's vegetarian.
**Waiter:** Yes, there are some. There's a vegetable curry, and there are some great veggie burgers.
**Priya:** The curry, please.
**Lucía:** And can I have fish and chips?
**Waiter:** Of course. Anything else?
**Sam:** Some bread for the table, please!
**Waiter:** Lovely. That's thirty-two pounds fifty, please.

## 6. Video call with Javi
`unit: my_week`

> Sunday evening. Lucía is in her room. Her brother Javi is on a video call from Valencia. He's a student, and his English exam is in December.

**Javi:** ¡Hola, Lucía!
**Lucía:** No, Javi. English only, remember?
**Javi:** OK, OK! Hello! How's your new job?
**Lucía:** I love it. I get up at seven and I go to work by bus.
**Javi:** By bus? You don't walk?
**Lucía:** No, I don't. The office is far. I start at nine and I finish at half past five.
**Javi:** And your flatmates? Do you eat together?
**Lucía:** Yes, we do. We have dinner together on Fridays.
**Javi:** What do you do in the evenings?
**Lucía:** I study English and I watch TV with Sam and Priya. And you?
**Javi:** I don't study. I play video games!
**Lucía:** Javi! Your exam is in December!
**Javi:** I know, I know…

## 7. Priya's shifts
`unit: shift_work`

> It's six o'clock in the morning. Lucía is awake, and there's a light in the kitchen. Priya is there with a cup of tea.

**Lucía:** Good morning, Priya! You're up early!
**Priya:** I always get up at half past five. I work at the hospital today.
**Lucía:** Do you work every day?
**Priya:** No. I usually work four days a week. Sometimes I work at night.
**Lucía:** At night? That's hard.
**Priya:** Yes, it is. When I work at night, I sleep in the day.
**Lucía:** And Sam? Does he get up early?
**Priya:** Sam? Never! He never gets up before ten. He works at the pub, and he finishes at midnight.
**Lucía:** Does he work at the pub every day?
**Priya:** No, he doesn't. He works there on Thursdays, Fridays and Saturdays. He often plays with his band on Fridays.
**Lucía:** And what does he do on Mondays?
**Priya:** He sleeps!

## 8. Where's the supermarket?
`unit: around_town`

> Saturday morning. Lucía needs some food, but where is the supermarket? She walks near the harbour and she sees a friendly face.

**Daniel:** Lucía! Hi! What's the craic?
**Lucía:** Sorry? The… craic?
**Daniel:** Ha, sorry. It's Irish. It means "How are you?"
**Lucía:** Oh! I'm fine. But I'm lost. Is there a supermarket near here?
**Daniel:** Yes, there's one on Park Street. Go straight on, then turn left at the bank.
**Lucía:** Straight on, then left at the bank…
**Daniel:** That's it. The supermarket is next to a café, opposite the cinema.
**Lucía:** Is there a chemist's near here too?
**Daniel:** Yes. It's between the post office and the bookshop.
**Lucía:** Thanks, Daniel. You're very kind.
**Daniel:** Ah, sure it's no bother. Come with me. I want a coffee from that café!

## 9. Friday night music
`unit: free_time`

> Friday night at The Old Duke. Sam's band plays here every Friday. Tonight Lucía is with Priya and Daniel.

**Daniel:** This place is great! Do you like live music, Lucía?
**Lucía:** I love it! And I love dancing too.
**Priya:** I don't like dancing. I like watching!
**Daniel:** What do you do in your free time, Priya?
**Priya:** I love cooking, and I like gardening. Our tomatoes are fantastic.
**Lucía:** And you, Daniel?
**Daniel:** I like running, and I love reading. But I hate shopping!
**Sam:** Hi, everyone! Do you like the band?
**Lucía:** We love it! You're brilliant, Sam!
**Sam:** Thanks! Daniel, nice to meet you. Lucía talks about you a lot.
**Daniel:** Really? Well, she talks about you too. And about your big black umbrella!

## 10. Football in the park
`unit: in_the_park`

> Sunday afternoon in Brandon Hill park. People from Harbour Studio play football here every month. Today Sam is with them too.

**Daniel:** Can you play football, Lucía?
**Lucía:** Yes, I can! I can't run very fast, but I can play.
**Helen:** Lucía, you're on my team. Daniel, you're on Sam's team.
**Sam:** Can I be the goalkeeper? I can't kick the ball very well!
**Daniel:** Of course you can.
**Lucía:** Goal!
**Daniel:** Wow! You can play really well.
**Lucía:** I play every week in Valencia with my friends.
**Sam:** Helen, can we stop now? I can't breathe!
**Helen:** Ha! OK, five minutes. Who can bring some water?
**Daniel:** I can. Lucía, can you help me?
**Lucía:** Yes, of course.

## 11. A rainy Saturday
`unit: rainy_day`

> It's Saturday morning and it's raining. It's cold and grey. Lucía is in the kitchen with a coffee. Javi is on the phone.

**Javi:** Hi, Lucía! What are you doing?
**Lucía:** I'm looking at the rain! It's raining again.
**Javi:** It's sunny here. I'm wearing shorts and a T-shirt!
**Lucía:** Lucky you! I'm wearing a jumper, jeans and thick socks.
**Javi:** Where are Sam and Priya?
**Lucía:** Priya is working today. And Sam is sleeping, of course. He always sleeps on Saturday mornings.
**Javi:** And Daniel? The Irish guy?
**Lucía:** Javi! I don't know. He usually goes running on Saturdays… in the rain!
**Javi:** Are you smiling, Lucía?
**Lucía:** No, I'm not! Oh, wait. Sam is coming into the kitchen. He's carrying his guitar.
**Sam:** Morning! Who are you talking to?
**Lucía:** My brother. Say hello!
**Sam:** Hiya, Javi! Bring the sun to Bristol, please!

## 12. How was your weekend?
`unit: last_weekend`

> Monday morning. Lucía is at the office. It's her third week in Bristol, and her English is better every day.

**Daniel:** Morning, Lucía! How was your weekend?
**Lucía:** It was great, thanks. On Saturday I went to the market with Priya.
**Daniel:** The market by the harbour? What did you buy?
**Lucía:** I bought some cheese and some flowers. Then we cooked a big lunch.
**Daniel:** Lovely. And on Sunday?
**Lucía:** On Sunday I visited the Clifton Suspension Bridge. It was beautiful, but it was very windy!
**Daniel:** Were you there alone?
**Lucía:** No, I wasn't. Sam was with me. We walked for three hours. And you? What did you do?
**Daniel:** I watched the rugby with my friends, and I phoned my mam in Galway. She asked about you!
**Lucía:** About me?
**Daniel:** Well… I talked about my new colleague from Spain. That's all!
