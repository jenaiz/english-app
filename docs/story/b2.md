# B2: *Conflicts*

Cuarto nivel de la historia (ver [bible.md](bible.md)). Va de octubre del segundo año a junio del tercero: el congreso de Edimburgo, un cliente difícil (Mark Reed), tensiones en Clifton Road con Ollie, el disco de Sam, la decisión de Lucía y Daniel de vivir juntos y el anuncio de que Helen se jubila.

**Formato**: el mismo que en [a1.md](a1.md) y [b1.md](b1.md).

---

## 1. The night train
`unit: conference_nerves`

> October, year two. Lucía and Daniel are on the train to Edinburgh. Her talk at the European Design Forum is tomorrow morning, and she hasn't stopped checking her slides since they left Bristol.

**Daniel:** Will you put that laptop away? You've read those slides about fifty times.
**Lucía:** I can't help it. This time tomorrow, I'll be standing in front of four hundred designers.
**Daniel:** And by eleven o'clock, you'll have finished, and we'll be having a pint to celebrate.
**Lucía:** Unless I've forgotten everything by then.
**Daniel:** You won't. What time will we be arriving, anyway?
**Lucía:** The train gets in at six. By the time we check in, it'll be dark.
**Daniel:** Grand. I'll have sent the demo to the organisers by then, so that's one less thing to worry about.
**Lucía:** Will you be sitting in the front row tomorrow?
**Daniel:** I'll be sitting at the back, controlling the screen. If anything goes wrong, I'll be there.
**Lucía:** Thank you. I'd be lost without you, you know.
**Daniel:** I know. Now, are you going to eat something, or are you too nervous?
**Lucía:** Too nervous. But I'll be starving by the time we get there.

## 2. After the talk
`unit: the_talk`

> The next morning, Lucía's talk goes well. Afterwards, an American man in an expensive suit is waiting for her near the stage.

**Mark:** Ms Martín? Mark Reed, Reed Hotels. That was a terrific talk.
**Lucía:** Thank you, Mr Reed. Nice to meet you.
**Mark:** Call me Mark. I'd like Harbour Studio to redesign our websites. All forty-two hotels.
**Lucía:** Forty-two? That's… a very big project.
**Mark:** Here's my card. Let's talk next week.
**Daniel:** Who was that? He must be important. Did you see his watch?
**Lucía:** He owns a hotel chain. He must have a lot of money.
**Daniel:** Reed Hotels? That can't be the company from the TV adverts. They're enormous.
**Lucía:** It might be. He said forty-two hotels.
**Daniel:** Then this could be the biggest contract Harbour Studio has ever had.
**Lucía:** Helen will be delighted. Or terrified. It could be either.
**Daniel:** She must be waiting for news. Call her now!

## 3. The missing laptop
`unit: missing_laptop`

> That evening, back at the hotel, Daniel can't find his laptop. It has the demo for Mark's project on it.

**Daniel:** It's gone. My laptop's gone.
**Lucía:** It can't have disappeared. You must have left it somewhere.
**Daniel:** I had it at the conference. I must have put it down when we were talking to Mark.
**Lucía:** Or you might have left it in the taxi.
**Daniel:** No, I can't have left it in the taxi. I remember holding it when we got out.
**Lucía:** Then someone at the conference must have picked it up by mistake.
**Receptionist:** Excuse me, Mr O'Brien? Somebody from the conference centre has just dropped this off for you.
**Daniel:** My laptop! Where did they find it?
**Receptionist:** Apparently it was under a chair in the main hall.
**Lucía:** So you must have left it there after all.
**Daniel:** I could have lost the whole demo. Thank goodness for honest people.
**Lucía:** You should have kept it in your bag.
**Daniel:** I know, I know. You don't have to say it.

## 4. The first meeting
`unit: expressing_opinions`

> November. Mark Reed is in Bristol for the first meeting with Harbour Studio. He isn't sure about Lucía's designs.

**Mark:** Although I like the colours, the homepage feels too simple.
**Lucía:** I understand. However, our research shows that guests want to book quickly.
**Mark:** Despite that, I think luxury needs more photos and more text.
**Helen:** If I may, Lucía has hit the nail on the head. Simple sells.
**Mark:** Well, it seems we don't see eye to eye on this.
**Daniel:** In spite of being simple, the design is much faster. Your current site takes ten seconds to load, whereas this one takes two.
**Mark:** Two seconds? That's interesting. Helen, what's your opinion? You can't sit on the fence forever.
**Helen:** I'm not sitting on the fence. I agree with Lucía, even though you're paying the bill.
**Mark:** Ha! I like honesty. Nevertheless, I need to see two versions.
**Lucía:** That's fair. We'll prepare a simple version and a richer one.
**Mark:** Deal.

## 5. The phone call
`unit: reporting_verbs`

> A week later, Mark calls Lucía. Afterwards, she goes to Helen's office to tell her about the conversation.

**Helen:** Well? What did he say?
**Lucía:** He admitted that the simple version was better.
**Helen:** Really? That's a first.
**Lucía:** But he insisted on adding a video to every page.
**Helen:** Every page? Did you warn him that it would slow the site down?
**Lucía:** Yes, I warned him about it. He refused to listen at first.
**Helen:** Typical. What happened then?
**Lucía:** Daniel suggested testing both versions with real guests.
**Helen:** Good idea. Did Mark agree?
**Lucía:** Eventually. He promised to send us the results by Friday. And he invited us to go to New York for the launch.
**Helen:** New York! Did he offer to pay for the flights?
**Lucía:** He offered to pay for everything. He even apologised for being so difficult.
**Helen:** Mark Reed apologised? I don't believe it.

## 6. The wrong version
`unit: wrong_version`

> December. The morning of the launch, Helen opens the new website and sees something strange: it's the old version.

**Daniel:** I can't believe it. We uploaded the old version.
**Lucía:** If we had checked the files, this wouldn't have happened.
**Daniel:** If I hadn't been so tired last night, I would have noticed.
**Helen:** It's not the end of the world. If Mark had seen it, he would have called us already.
**Lucía:** I wish I had stayed later yesterday.
**Daniel:** And I wish I hadn't uploaded it in such a hurry.
**Helen:** Stop it, both of you. Fix it now, and nobody will know.
**Daniel:** Done. The right version is live.
**Lucía:** That was close. If you hadn't spotted it this morning, Helen, we'd have been in real trouble.
**Helen:** Twenty years in this business, Lucía. You learn to check everything twice.
**Mark:** Hi, team! The site looks amazing. Did I miss anything?
**Lucía:** Nothing at all, Mark. Everything's fine.

## 7. Night shifts
`unit: house_tensions`

> January, year three. Ollie has been working late at the restaurant, and life at Clifton Road has changed.

**Priya:** I wish Ollie wouldn't cook at two in the morning. I could hear the pans from my room.
**Lucía:** I know. And I wish he'd clean up afterwards. The kitchen was a disaster this morning.
**Priya:** To be fair, the food he leaves is amazing.
**Lucía:** True. I wish I could cook like him.
**Ollie:** Morning, both. Is something wrong? You look tired.
**Priya:** Ollie, we need to talk. I wish you'd be a bit quieter at night.
**Ollie:** Oh no. Have I been waking you up? I'm so sorry.
**Lucía:** And the kitchen…
**Ollie:** I wish I'd known earlier! Why didn't you say something?
**Priya:** We didn't want to upset you.
**Ollie:** You wouldn't have upset me. From now on, I'll cook quietly and clean everything. Promise.
**Lucía:** But you can still leave us the leftovers.
**Ollie:** Deal. If only all problems were this easy.

## 8. The boiler
`unit: get_it_fixed`

> February. The boiler at Clifton Road breaks down during the coldest week of the year. Mrs Green comes round.

**Mrs Green:** I'm having the boiler repaired on Thursday. The engineer can't come any earlier.
**Priya:** Thursday? We haven't had hot water since Sunday!
**Mrs Green:** I know, dear. And while I'm at it, I'm getting the kitchen repainted next month.
**Ollie:** Could you have the oven checked too? It's been making a strange noise.
**Mrs Green:** Fine. I'll get someone to look at it.
**Lucía:** Meanwhile, I'm going to shower at the gym. I might get my hair cut while I'm there.
**Priya:** Good plan. I had my car serviced last week, so at least the heating in the car works.
**Mrs Green:** You young people. In my day, we had the chimney swept and lit a fire.
**Ollie:** Shall I make some soup to keep us warm?
**Mrs Green:** Now that's the best idea I've heard all day.

## 9. The reviews
`unit: the_reviews`

> March. Sam's album is out, and Priya is reading the reviews aloud in the kitchen.

**Priya:** Listen to this: "The Clifton Cats' first album is said to be the surprise of the year."
**Lucía:** The surprise of the year! Sam must be over the moon.
**Priya:** There's more. "The band is believed to have signed a deal for a European tour."
**Ollie:** A European tour? Is it true?
**Priya:** It's being reported everywhere. Apparently they're expected to play in Madrid in June.
**Lucía:** Madrid! We have to go. Javi could fly from Dublin.
**Ollie:** What else does it say?
**Priya:** "The singer, Sam Turner, is thought to have written most of the songs in a small house in Bristol."
**Lucía:** That's our house! Our kitchen is famous.
**Ollie:** It's also well known that he never cleaned it.
**Priya:** Ha! It says the album was recorded in just six weeks.
**Lucía:** Let's call him. He's supposed to be in London this week.

## 10. A question at the harbour
`unit: moving_in`

> April. Lucía and Daniel are walking along the harbour on a Sunday afternoon.

**Daniel:** Lucía, I've been meaning to ask you something.
**Lucía:** That sounds serious. Go on.
**Daniel:** Would you consider moving in with me? My contract ends in June.
**Lucía:** Oh! I'd love to… but I'd miss living with Priya.
**Daniel:** I remember you saying that the house was the best thing about Bristol.
**Lucía:** It was, at the beginning. But now I can't imagine not seeing you every day.
**Daniel:** So is that a yes?
**Lucía:** It's a yes. But you'll have to stop leaving your socks everywhere.
**Daniel:** I'll try to be tidier. I promise.
**Lucía:** And I need to tell Priya. I regret not telling her that we were thinking about it.
**Daniel:** She'll be happy for you. She suggested looking for flats together last month, remember?
**Lucía:** That's true! I'd forgotten about that.
**Daniel:** Shall we stop to get a coffee and start looking online?

## 11. On the bridge
`unit: what_if`

> May. Javi is visiting Bristol from Dublin for the first time. He and Lucía are standing on the Clifton Suspension Bridge, looking down at the river.

**Javi:** I can't believe you've lived here for almost three years.
**Lucía:** Me neither. If I hadn't taken that job, I wouldn't be here now.
**Javi:** And you wouldn't be moving in with Daniel!
**Lucía:** True. And if you hadn't passed your exam, you wouldn't be working in Dublin.
**Javi:** If I weren't so lazy, I would have passed it the first time!
**Lucía:** Do you ever wonder what would have happened if I'd stayed in Madrid?
**Javi:** You'd be bored, and you'd still speak terrible English.
**Lucía:** Thanks a lot!
**Javi:** Seriously, if you weren't so brave, you would never have left Spain.
**Lucía:** I don't feel brave. I just wanted an adventure.
**Javi:** And now? What's the next adventure?
**Lucía:** Actually… I've been thinking about starting my own design studio.
**Javi:** Your own studio? Does Helen know?
**Lucía:** Not yet. That's the next difficult conversation.

## 12. Big news
`unit: big_news`

> June. Helen calls the whole team into the meeting room. She looks emotional.

**Helen:** I have some news. Harbour Studio, which I started twenty years ago in my kitchen, is going to change.
**Daniel:** Change how?
**Helen:** I'm retiring at the end of the year. My husband, who has been very patient, wants us to travel.
**Lucía:** Helen, that's wonderful. But what will happen to the studio?
**Helen:** That's the question. I've had an offer from Mark Reed, whose company wants to buy us.
**Daniel:** Mark Reed? Our Mark?
**Helen:** Yes. But there's another option, which I prefer. Lucía, can I speak to you after the meeting?
**Helen:** Daniel tells me you've been thinking about starting your own studio, which doesn't surprise me at all.
**Lucía:** He told you? Oh no… I was going to tell you myself.
**Helen:** Don't worry. I'd like you to take over some of our clients, most of whom already ask for you by name.
**Lucía:** Me? Take over your clients?
**Helen:** Think about it. You'd have your own studio, and the work I care about would be in good hands.
**Lucía:** I… I don't know what to say.
**Helen:** Say you'll think about it. That's all I ask.
