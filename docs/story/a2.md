# A2: *Settling in*

Segundo nivel de la historia (ver [bible.md](bible.md)). Va de octubre a diciembre del primer año: Lucía se asienta en Bristol, compara su vida con la de Valencia, viaja a Londres, se pone enferma, recibe una invitación de Daniel en la fiesta de Navidad y vuelve a casa por vacaciones.

**Formato**: el mismo que en [a1.md](a1.md).

---

## 1. A busy week
`unit: daily_routine`

> It's Tuesday morning, early October. The kitchen at Clifton Road is very busy today.

**Priya:** Lucía, what are you doing? You're wearing my slippers!
**Lucía:** Oh no, sorry! I'm looking for my keys, and I can't find my shoes.
**Priya:** You usually leave your keys on the table by the door.
**Lucía:** I know, but they aren't there today. Is Sam using them?
**Sam:** Nope. I'm making toast. I never touch other people's keys!
**Priya:** Well, you usually take my milk…
**Sam:** That's different! Anyway, why are you running around? You don't start work until nine.
**Lucía:** This week I'm starting at eight. We're working on a big project for a new client.
**Priya:** Here they are! Your keys are in the fridge.
**Lucía:** In the fridge? I need more sleep!
**Sam:** You need a coffee. Here, take this one. I'm not drinking it.

## 2. A coat for the winter
`unit: at_the_shop`

> On Saturday, Lucía and Priya go to Cabot Circus, a big shopping centre in the city. Lucía needs a warm coat for the winter.

**Priya:** How much money do you want to spend?
**Lucía:** Not much! I don't have a lot of money this month.
**Shop assistant:** Hello! Can I help you?
**Lucía:** Yes, please. I'm looking for a winter coat. How much is this blue one?
**Shop assistant:** It's eighty-five pounds. But there are a lot of coats in the sale over there.
**Lucía:** Can I try this one on?
**Shop assistant:** Of course. What size are you?
**Lucía:** Medium, I think.
**Priya:** How many coats do you want to try?
**Lucía:** Just two or three! Oh, it's too big. Have you got it in a small?
**Shop assistant:** Let me check… Yes, here you are.
**Lucía:** Perfect. And it's only forty pounds in the sale!
**Priya:** Great. Now we've got some money for a hot chocolate.

## 3. Old photos
`unit: past_memories`

> One rainy Sunday evening, Sam finds Lucía on the sofa with some old photos from Valencia.

**Sam:** Who's that little girl with the big ice cream?
**Lucía:** That's me! I was six. We went to the beach every summer.
**Sam:** Did you live near the sea?
**Lucía:** Yes, we did. Our flat was ten minutes from the beach.
**Sam:** Lucky you! I grew up in Bristol, so I didn't see the sea very often.
**Lucía:** Did you play music when you were a child?
**Sam:** Yes. My dad gave me a guitar when I was eight. I didn't practise much, but I loved it.
**Lucía:** And did you go to university?
**Sam:** No, I didn't. I started working in a pub when I was eighteen. What about you?
**Lucía:** I studied design in Valencia. Then I worked in Madrid for three years.
**Sam:** Why did you leave Spain?
**Lucía:** I wanted a new adventure. And my English was terrible!
**Sam:** Well, it isn't terrible now.

## 4. What a morning!
`unit: a_bad_day`

> Monday morning, late October. Lucía arrives at Harbour Studio twenty minutes late. Her hair is wet and there's coffee on her new coat.

**Daniel:** Lucía! Are you OK? What happened?
**Lucía:** What a morning! I was walking to the bus stop when it started to rain.
**Daniel:** Oh no. Didn't you have your umbrella?
**Lucía:** Yes, but while I was opening it, the wind broke it!
**Daniel:** Sam's famous black umbrella? Not again!
**Lucía:** Then, when I was getting on the bus, a man ran into me and my coffee went everywhere.
**Daniel:** That's terrible. And what were you doing when Helen called? She was looking for you at nine.
**Lucía:** I was sitting on the bus. I couldn't hear my phone.
**Daniel:** Don't worry. She wasn't angry. She was talking to a client all morning.
**Lucía:** Thank goodness. Is there any coffee in the kitchen?
**Daniel:** Sit down. I can make you one.

## 5. Bristol or Valencia?
`unit: comparing_cities`

> Javi calls on Sunday. He wants to practise his English for his exam, and he has a lot of questions about Bristol.

**Javi:** So, which city is better, Bristol or Valencia?
**Lucía:** That's a difficult question! Valencia is bigger and sunnier, of course.
**Javi:** And the food?
**Lucía:** The food in Valencia is better. But Bristol is more interesting than I thought. There's a lot of music and art.
**Javi:** Is it more expensive?
**Lucía:** Yes, much more expensive. My rent here is higher than in Madrid.
**Javi:** And the people? Are they friendlier than in Spain?
**Lucía:** They're quieter, but they're very kind. And they say "sorry" more often than we do!
**Javi:** Ha! And the weather is worse, right?
**Lucía:** Much worse. It's colder and wetter. But Bristol isn't as grey as people say.
**Javi:** Good friends make it better… like the Irish guy?
**Lucía:** Javi! Daniel is just a friend. And he's funnier than you!

## 6. Quiz night
`unit: the_best_pub`

> It's Thursday, quiz night at The Old Duke. Lucía, Sam, Priya and Daniel are a team. Their name is "The Clifton Brains".

**Quizmaster:** Question five. What is the longest river in the world?
**Daniel:** The Nile, I think. Or is it the Amazon?
**Priya:** The Nile. Write it down!
**Quizmaster:** Question six. Which is the biggest city in Ireland?
**Daniel:** Easy! Dublin. It's the biggest and the most expensive.
**Sam:** And Galway is the best, right?
**Daniel:** Of course. It's the most beautiful place in the world!
**Quizmaster:** Question seven. What is the most popular hot drink in Britain?
**Lucía:** Tea! Priya drinks the most tea in the house.
**Priya:** That's not true. Sam drinks the most coffee!
**Quizmaster:** And the winners tonight are… The Clifton Brains!
**Sam:** Yes! This is the best night of the year!
**Lucía:** And I won something in English for the first time!

## 7. A plan for London
`unit: london_plans`

> November. Priya has some free days, and Daniel has a friend in London. At lunch in a café near the harbour, they make a plan.

**Daniel:** So, are we going to London next weekend or not?
**Lucía:** Yes! I'm going to book the train tickets tonight.
**Priya:** Where are we going to stay?
**Daniel:** My friend Ciarán has a flat in Camden. We're going to sleep there. It's small, but it's free!
**Priya:** Perfect. What are we going to do on Saturday?
**Lucía:** I'm going to visit the British Museum. I really want to see it.
**Daniel:** And I'm going to take you to a great market in Camden.
**Priya:** I'm not going to walk all day. My feet always hurt after work!
**Daniel:** Don't worry, we're going to use the Tube.
**Lucía:** Is Sam going to come?
**Priya:** No, he isn't. His band is going to play at a wedding.
**Daniel:** Then it's the three of us. London, here we come!

## 8. At the station
`unit: at_the_station`

> Saturday morning at Bristol Temple Meads station. Lucía has the tickets on her phone, but there's a problem.

**Lucía:** Oh no! The tickets are for next Saturday, not today!
**Priya:** What? OK, don't panic. I'll go and ask at the ticket office.
**Agent:** Good morning. How can I help?
**Priya:** We've got tickets for the wrong day. Can we change them?
**Agent:** Let me see… Yes, you can, but it will cost ten pounds each.
**Lucía:** That's fine. I'll pay. It was my mistake.
**Daniel:** Then I'll pay for the coffees.
**Agent:** The next train to London leaves at nine fifteen from platform five.
**Priya:** Will it be busy?
**Agent:** I think it will be quite busy. It's Saturday.
**Daniel:** We won't find three seats together, then.
**Lucía:** Never mind. I'll stand. I'm too happy to sit down!

## 9. A day in London
`unit: in_london`

> Saturday afternoon in London. The three friends are walking along the river near Tower Bridge.

**Daniel:** Have you ever been to London before, Lucía?
**Lucía:** No, never. I've seen it in films, but I've never been here.
**Priya:** I've been here many times. My aunt lives in Wembley.
**Daniel:** Have you ever tried pie and mash?
**Lucía:** No, I haven't. What is it?
**Daniel:** It's a very old London dish. I've eaten it once. I didn't like it!
**Priya:** Have you seen the Tower of London? It's just there.
**Lucía:** Wow! It's beautiful. I've taken a hundred photos today.
**Daniel:** Javi will want all of them!
**Priya:** Have you two ever been on the London Eye?
**Daniel:** I have. I went on it last year, with my mam.
**Lucía:** I haven't. Can we go?
**Priya:** Of course. Let's go!

## 10. Feeling ill
`unit: feeling_ill`

> December. Lucía wakes up with a sore throat and a headache. Priya comes home after a night shift at the hospital.

**Priya:** You look terrible! What's wrong?
**Lucía:** I've got a cold, I think. My throat hurts and I've got a headache.
**Priya:** You should go back to bed. And you shouldn't go to work today.
**Lucía:** But I have to finish the designs for the new client.
**Priya:** You don't have to finish them today. Helen will understand.
**Lucía:** Should I take something?
**Priya:** Take some paracetamol and drink a lot of water. Hot tea with honey and lemon is good too.
**Lucía:** Do I have to see a doctor?
**Priya:** No, you don't. It's just a cold. But you have to rest.
**Sam:** I'm going to make you my grandma's chicken soup. It's the best medicine in Bristol!
**Lucía:** Thank you both. You're the best flatmates in the world.

## 11. The Christmas party
`unit: christmas_party`

> Friday, the office Christmas party. Harbour Studio is full of lights and music, and Helen is making a short speech.

**Helen:** Thank you all for a fantastic year. I'd like to welcome our newest designer, Lucía. Her work is excellent.
**Lucía:** Thank you, Helen. I'm very happy to be here.
**Daniel:** Would you like a drink, Lucía?
**Lucía:** Yes, I'd like a glass of wine, please.
**Daniel:** So, are you going home for Christmas?
**Lucía:** Yes. I decided to fly on the twentieth. I want to see my family, and I need some sun!
**Daniel:** I'm going to Galway. My mam wants to know everything about Bristol.
**Daniel:** Lucía, would you like to have dinner with me after Christmas? Just the two of us.
**Lucía:** I'd love to.
**Daniel:** Really? Grand! I'd like to take you to a little Irish restaurant I know.
**Lucía:** And now, do you want to dance?
**Daniel:** I can't dance… but I'd like to try!

## 12. Home for Christmas
`unit: home_for_christmas`

> It's the twentieth of December. Lucía's flight to Valencia leaves at two o'clock, and it's already eleven.

**Priya:** Have you packed yet?
**Lucía:** Yes, I've already packed my clothes. But I haven't found my passport yet!
**Sam:** Have you looked in the fridge? Your keys were there in October!
**Lucía:** Very funny, Sam. Oh, here it is! It was in my coat.
**Priya:** Have you checked in online?
**Lucía:** Yes, I've just done it. And I've already ordered a taxi.
**Sam:** We've got something for you. Open it!
**Lucía:** A new umbrella! A red one! Thank you!
**Priya:** Now you've got your own umbrella. No more broken black ones!
**Sam:** Has Daniel called you today?
**Lucía:** He's just sent me a message. He wants a photo of the sea!
**Priya:** The taxi has just arrived. Go! Merry Christmas!
**Lucía:** Merry Christmas! See you in January!
